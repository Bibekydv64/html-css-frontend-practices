FLASK MASTERY ROADMAP
Beginner to Freelance-Ready Backend Developer
=============================================

16-DAY FIRST SPRINT + DAY 17
============================

Day 1: GET, POST, request.form, form validation
Day 2: Jinja2, template inheritance, redirects, flash messages

Day 3: SQL basics and schema design

Day 4: SQLAlchemy CRUD and relationships

Day 5: Registration, password hashing, sessions, roles

Day 6: Email integration overview, error handling, testing basics

Days 7-9: Student Management System

Days 10-11: Inventory Management System
Days 12-13: Employee roles, email verification, reports
Days 14-15: E-commerce catalog, cart, orders (initial implementation)
Day 16: Test, fix, document, and deploy the best-completed project
Day 17: Prepare freelance profile, portfolio, and client proposals

This sprint is deliberately ambitious. Continue practicing after day 17.
Do not rush security, testing, or deployment merely to meet the calendar.


START HERE — FIRST CHALLENGE
============================
Build a student registration form using Flask.
Requirements:
1. GET /register displays the form.
2. POST /register processes name, email, and age.
3. Missing fields must be rejected.
4. Age must be a valid positive integer.
5. Display success or validation errors.
6. Keep data in memory for now; no database yet.
7. Test valid, empty, and malformed inputs.
GOAL
----
Build strong Flask and backend fundamentals through ordered topics, exercises,
mini-projects, freelance-style projects, testing, and deployment.
